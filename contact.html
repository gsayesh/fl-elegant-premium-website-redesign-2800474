Questions

Before you enquire.

Which areas do you build in?

Sydney’s North Shore, Northern Beaches, Lower North Shore, Eastern Suburbs and Inner West. For exceptional projects we travel further.

Do I need an architect already?

No. Our in-house design studio can take you from concept to approval, or we can work with an architect you have chosen.

What does a custom home cost?

Most of our new homes fall between $4,500 and $7,500 per square metre depending on site, design and finishes. We give an honest range at your first meeting.

How long does the build take?

Typically 9–14 months on site for a new home, and 6–9 months for major renovations, once approvals are in place.
