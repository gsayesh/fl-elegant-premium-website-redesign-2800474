05

Imagery

The concept uses drawn architectural illustrations so nothing reads as stock. Your photography replaces them one-for-one.

  • Hero: 4 : 3.4 frame with an arched top (220px radius). Exterior at golden hour, the house centred and low in the frame.
  • Project cards: 3 : 2, front elevation or a three-quarter view; 2400px wide WebP/JPG.
  • Case-study drawings: elevations or plans in a white mount with a 12px border (they reinforce craft and the tender process).
  • Grade: warm, natural, slightly desaturated; avoid blue skies and saturated greens.
