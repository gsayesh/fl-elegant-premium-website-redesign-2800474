How we work

Five clear stages. No surprises.

You will always know what happens next, what it costs and who is responsible. Our tender is itemised line by line, so you can see exactly where every dollar goes.

  1. Consult

    We walk your site, listen to your brief and talk honestly about budget and timing.

    Week 1

  2. Design

    Concept sketches become a resolved design with our architects or yours.

    Weeks 2–10

  3. Tender

    A detailed, itemised take-off and a fixed-price HIA contract you can trust.

    Weeks 10–14

  4. Build

    One supervisor, weekly site walks and a private client portal with photo updates.

    9–14 months

  5. Handover

    A detailed walkthrough, a home manual and our 12-month care visit.

    And beyond
