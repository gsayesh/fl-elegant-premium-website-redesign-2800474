Home  /  About & Process

A builder’s craft, an architect’s eye.

Founded in 2004 by a carpenter and an architect, Ellery & Stone remains deliberately small: we start no more than twelve homes a year, so every one receives our full attention.

Our story

Small by design.

We have spent two decades building the kind of homes we would want to live in ourselves. Our carpenters, stonemasons and joiners have worked with us for years, and many of our clients come to us through their neighbours.

We price every project from a detailed quantity take-off, not a rate per square metre, so the contract you sign reflects the home you have designed — down to the last hinge.

What we value

Three promises we keep.

Transparent pricing

Itemised tenders, fixed-price contracts and no provisional sums hiding in the fine print.

Built to last

Honest materials, careful detailing and trades who take pride in work that will outlive them.

One team, start to finish

The same supervisor from your first site walk to the twelve-month care visit.

Our process

From first sketch to first night home.

Most custom homes take 14 to 20 months from first meeting to handover. Here is how that time is spent.

  1. Consult

    Site visit, brief, feasibility and an honest budget conversation. No cost, no obligation.

    Week 1

  2. Design

    Concept, design development and documentation, ready for council or CDC approval.

    Weeks 2–10

  3. Tender

    A quantity surveyor’s take-off becomes your itemised, fixed-price HIA contract.

    Weeks 10–14

  4. Build

    Weekly site meetings, a client portal with photos and a programme you can follow.

    9–14 months

  5. Handover

    Final walkthrough, home manual, warranties and a care visit twelve months on.

    And beyond
