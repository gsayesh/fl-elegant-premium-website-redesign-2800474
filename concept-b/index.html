04 / Process

Five stages.
No surprises.

Our tender is a full quantity take-off, itemised line by line, so the contract you sign reflects the home you designed — down to the last hinge.

  1. i.

    Consult Week 1

    We walk your site, listen to the brief and talk honestly about budget and timing.

  2. ii.

    Design Weeks 2–10

    Concept sketches grow into a resolved, approval-ready design with our architects or yours.

  3. iii.

    Tender Weeks 10–14

    An itemised take-off becomes a fixed-price HIA contract you can read and trust.

  4. iv.

    Build 9–14 months

    One supervisor, weekly site walks and a private portal with photo updates.

  5. v.

    Handover And beyond

    A considered walkthrough, your home manual and a care visit twelve months on.
