About / Since 2004

Small by design.

Founded by a carpenter and an architect, Ellery & Stone starts no more than twelve new homes a year — so every client has a director’s phone number and every site has our full attention.

We build for the long evening — the hour when the light goes golden, the timber warms and a house quietly becomes a home. Everything we do is in service of that hour.

Our story / Milestones

Twenty-two years, one street at a time.

Our carpenters, stonemasons and joiners have worked with us for years. Many of our clients find us through their neighbours — the highest compliment a builder can receive.

Process / 14–20 months

From first sketch to first night home.

You will always know what happens next, what it costs and who is responsible.

Start with a consultation →
  1. i.

    Consult Week 1

    Site visit, brief, feasibility and an honest conversation about budget. No cost, no obligation.

  2. ii.

    Design Weeks 2–10

    Concept, design development and documentation, ready for council or CDC approval.

  3. iii.

    Tender Weeks 10–14

    A full quantity take-off becomes your itemised, fixed-price HIA contract.

  4. iv.

    Build 9–14 months

    Weekly site meetings, a private client portal and a programme you can follow.

  5. v.

    Handover And beyond

    Walkthrough, home manual, warranties and a care visit twelve months on.
