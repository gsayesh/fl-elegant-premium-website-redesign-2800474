05

Imagery

The whole concept is art-directed around dusk: warm interior light, deep sky, quiet landscape.

  • Hero: full-bleed dusk exterior, the house in the upper two-thirds so the headline sits on the dark foreground.
  • Project rail: 4 : 5 portrait crops; project features: 3 : 2.
  • Grade: blue-hour sky pulled towards umber; windows warm (around 2700K); no harsh flash.
  • Illustrations in assets/art/*-dusk.svg are drop-in placeholders until your photography is supplied.
